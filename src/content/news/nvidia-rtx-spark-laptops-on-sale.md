Picture a video editor or a developer who wants to run AI on their own machine, with no monthly fee and no files leaving the desk. From today, 16 October, buyers in the US can start getting an RTX Spark laptop, one of the first Windows laptops built around Nvidia's own main chip. The first independent reviews arrived a day earlier: mostly warm, with clear warnings about price and games.

Windows laptops have long run mostly on Intel or AMD chips, with Qualcomm a newer option. Nvidia, best known for graphics cards and data-centre AI chips, now wants the whole laptop. That gives buyers one more serious choice.

## One Nvidia chip in two sizes, from about US$2,600

The chip is called RTX Spark, model name N1X. It joins an Nvidia Blackwell graphics processor to an Nvidia Grace processor built on Arm, a chip design common in phones; MediaTek helped design it. There are two RTX Spark N1X versions: a 20-core processor with a 6,144-core graphics chip, and a cheaper 18-core version with a 5,120-core graphics chip.

Laptops carry up to 128 GB of shared memory, which Nvidia says can run AI models of up to 120 billion parameters on the laptop itself.

The RTX Spark laptop price starts high. These are the makers' own prices (checked on 16 October 2026):

- Microsoft Surface Laptop Ultra: from US$2,599.99 (£2,599 in the UK), up to US$5,899.99 with 128 GB
- HP OmniBook X 14 and OmniBook Ultra 16: US$2,999.99 and US$3,199.99
- Lenovo Yoga 9n 2-in-1: US$2,999.99
- ASUS ProArt P14 and P16: US$2,599.99 and US$2,799.99, on pre-order, due "this October"
- Dell XPS 16 Creator Edition: US$3,799.99, on pre-order at Best Buy

## Early RTX Spark review verdicts: strong at AI, weaker at play

Tom's Hardware tested the top Surface (20 cores, 128 GB). In its AI-agent test, Apple's M5 Max MacBook Pro took about a quarter longer, and AMD's Ryzen AI Max+ 395 about twice as long.

Windows Central said the graphics chip "really shines" but called the processor "just okay". It also found the Surface ran about as fast on battery as plugged in, and Engadget noticed no slowdown on battery while playing. Engadget put a 128 GB M5 Max MacBook Pro at about US$7,000, more than the US$5,900 128 GB Surface.

Is RTX Spark Arm based? Yes. Most games are written for Intel and AMD chips, so here they run through Prism, Microsoft's built-in translator, which costs some speed.

"All your apps, all your games, everything's going to run great on Spark," Justin Walker, Nvidia's senior director of products, told journalists, The Verge reports. That is Nvidia's claim, not yet a tested fact.

So, is RTX Spark good for gaming? It works, but it is not the main event. Windows Central saw close to 90 frames a second in The Witcher 3, yet Red Dead Redemption 2 crashed until a setting was changed. Tom's Hardware said gaming is "not the primary focus". Windows Central says most mainstream anti-cheat software, which many online games need, now works; Nvidia says it is working with the major anti-cheat makers.

## Before you buy, check your apps and your country

Only the US and the UK are confirmed so far; ASUS says availability differs by country. We found no official price or date for other countries yet.

If you run AI models privately or do video and 3D work, this is worth a closer look. If you mainly play online games, or have a normal budget, waiting is wiser. As Tom's Hardware noted, these prices still buy a lot of cloud AI.

Before buying, check that the apps you rely on run on Windows on Arm, especially anti-cheat games, VPNs, printers and older drivers.

## A promising first step for Windows, priced for professionals for now

The good is real. Independent tests back Nvidia's main promise on AI speed, performance held up on battery in early tests, and five big laptop makers have models on sale or pre-order, with MSI's coming soon. Engadget expects RTX Spark to push Intel and AMD, and that seems the likely result.

The limits are just as real: prices from about US$2,600, gaming that leans on translation, and unknown battery hours. As with Meta's [Muse Spark Open on two Mac Studios](/news/meta-muse-spark-open-mac-studio/), local AI is still priced for professionals.

On balance, RTX Spark is a meaningful step for Windows, and a sensible buy today mainly for people whose work centres on AI or creative apps. For most of us, the second generation is likely the one to watch.

Watch for battery tests, anti-cheat support and prices beyond the US and UK. The AI boom behind [TSMC's record quarter](/news/tsmc-q3-2026-record-profit/) has so far been about data centres; RTX Spark is an early, expensive test of whether it can move onto a laptop too.
