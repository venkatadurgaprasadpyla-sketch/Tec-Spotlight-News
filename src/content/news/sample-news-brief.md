## What happened

This is where the brief explains the news in two or three short paragraphs. Short sentences. One idea per paragraph.

## Why people are talking about it

This part covers the reaction: what users, experts or communities are saying. Rumours are always labelled clearly, like this: **Rumour:** an unconfirmed report says…

## What this means for you

- **In India:** what changes for students, young professionals or small businesses here, including prices in rupees where it helps.
- **In the UAE:** what changes for readers there, including prices in dirhams where it helps.
