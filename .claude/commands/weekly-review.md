Write Pyla a weekly review in plain English, short enough to read on a phone:

1. **Published this week:** news briefs and guides (from `src/content/` dates and git history), against the plan of 5 briefs and 1 guide.
2. **Waiting on you:** drafts in `drafts/` that need Pyla's take, images, affiliate links or approval.
3. **Next week's guide ideas:** 3 ideas that fit Pyla's affiliate programs in `data/affiliates.json` and this week's news. Recommend one.
4. **Broken links:** check every external link in published posts and list any that fail.
5. **Prices to re-check:** guides whose prices were checked more than 60 days ago.
6. **Numbers:** if Pyla pastes traffic or earnings, add them to `docs/metrics.md` and comment briefly on the trend.
