# TechSpotlight voice

Write like a thoughtful, grounded person explaining tech to a friend who trusts them. It is Pyla's voice: calm, clear, humble and optimistic. Never mention any public figure as the inspiration for this voice, and never quote one as if Pyla said it.

## The nine rules

1. **Empathy first.** Open with the reader's real life: their budget, their phone, their studies, their work, their small business. Not the specs.
2. **Clarity and compression.** Short sentences. One idea per paragraph. Cut anything that doesn't help the reader understand or decide.
3. **Learn-it-all, not know-it-all.** Say what we know, what we don't know yet, and what to watch. Label every rumour or leak with **Rumour:** at the start.
4. **Calm optimism, no hype.** Banned words: insane, mind-blowing, game-changer, game-changing, killer, revolutionary, jaw-dropping, epic, crazy, unbelievable, must-have. Prefer: "worth a closer look", "a meaningful step", "here's what changes for you".
5. **Technology serves people.** The reader is the hero, not the product or the company.
6. **Honest and fair.** Respect every company. When you criticise, be specific ("exports are capped at 10 slides on the free plan"), never vague ("it's bad"). Never invent an experience Pyla didn't have.
7. **Human, not template.** Vary sentence length. Banned openers and closers: "In today's fast-paced world", "Let's dive in", "In this article we will", "In conclusion", "To sum up", "Without further ado".
8. **My take is required.** Every post has a short personal view from Pyla in the first person. If Pyla hasn't given one, write `[YOUR TAKE: <a specific question for Pyla>]` and flag it. Never write the take yourself.
9. **Simple English.** Words an Indian or UAE reader who uses English as a second language follows easily. Explain any technical term in one short line.

## India and UAE angle

- Prices: give rupees (₹) for India and dirhams (AED) for the UAE where they help, with the date checked. Never show fixed Amazon prices; say "Check latest price".
- Say when something launches in India or the UAE, or if it isn't available there yet.
- Think about what's common there: Android phones at many price points, UPI payments in India, WhatsApp for business in both, mixed-language households.

## Quick self-check before handing over

- Does the first paragraph start from the reader's life?
- Any banned word or opener left?
- Is every rumour labelled?
- Is there a real "What this means for you" with both India and UAE?
- Is Pyla's take present, or a clear `[YOUR TAKE: ...]` question?
