# Top risks

*Draft for approval · 14 October 2026*

| # | Risk | Why it matters | What we'll do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Google sees the site as "thin" or mass-made AI content | Google can bury the site in search and AdSense can refuse it. | Every post needs the owner's real take and adds something the sources don't. The auditor checks for this before anything is shown for approval. |
| 2 | Affiliate programs reject a brand-new site | No income from the main source at first. | Apply only after a few good posts and the required pages are live. Have a backup tool for each guide. |
| 3 | Lower affiliate pay for Indian visitors | Some programs pay much less per signup from India than from richer countries. | Give the UAE its own angle in every post, and favour programs that pay a percentage of sales over a fixed per-country fee. |
| 4 | The owner's time runs short | Every post needs approval and a personal take, so posts pile up. | Start at 1 brief a day and 1 guide a week. Make approving a draft quick with a short "needs from you" checklist. |
| 5 | Facts or prices go out of date | Wrong prices hurt trust and break affiliate rules. | Show software prices with "checked on <date>", never fixed Amazon prices, and re-check guides during the weekly review. |
| 6 | Copyright problems with images or text | Legal takedowns, and AdSense refusal. | Only press-kit images, the owner's own screenshots, or Canva graphics. The auditor checks for copied sentences. |
| 7 | Something goes live by mistake | Unreviewed content on the public site. | Drafts never go on the live site. Publishing only happens after the owner says "publish". |
| 8 | Domain gets taken before it's bought | We'd need a new name and logo work. | Owner buys techspotlight.news soon. |
