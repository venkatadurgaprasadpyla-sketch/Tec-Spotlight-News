## How the ~US$825bn is made up (arithmetic checked)

- 454.52 + 355.73 = **810.25** (contracts + leases not yet started)
- + 14.72 contingent cloud-capacity obligation = **824.97 ≈ US$825bn**
- The ~US$825bn is **Bloomberg's sum** of these three items (TNW says so explicitly: "Bloomberg reported the total by adding together three items in the filing"). Meta's filing does not state a total.

## The "about US$700bn in July" comparison

- Bloomberg (via Yahoo) and TNW: "almost US$700bn" in July. Attributed to Bloomberg's count.
- Our check of Meta's Q2 10-Q (as of 30 Jun 2026): leases not yet started US$278.99bn + non-cancellable commitments US$349.31bn + contingent US$14.72bn = **US$643.02bn**. The Q2 10-Q also disclosed about **US$68bn** of extra data-centre leases signed in July 2026 (start 2027–2028, 18–20 year terms). 643 + 68 = ~711. So "almost 700" is a rough Bloomberg figure; the draft attributes it to Bloomberg and uses the filing's 30 June numbers for the like-for-like rise (~US$643bn → ~US$825bn on the same three items, about US$182bn more in one quarter).
- Non-cancellable commitments: 349.31 → 454.52 (+105.21). Leases not yet started: 278.99 → 355.73 (+76.74).
