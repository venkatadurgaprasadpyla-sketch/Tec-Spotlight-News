## Share-price reaction (corrections here)

- **Correction to the brief:** the "fell ~5-6% after hours" is only part of the picture. Shares had **already fallen ~6.4% in the regular session on 22 Oct** (close US$354.19 vs US$378.51; StockAnalysis/CNN; Investing.com) amid a wider AI/chip selloff, **before** the results.
- After hours: shares first rose ~4% on the numbers, then turned lower during the call. Size of the after-hours drop varies by source and time: ~4.9% (Investing.com transcript piece, ~US$337), ~5.6% (StockAnalysis, US$334.50), "about 8%" (Investing.com/Yahoo news piece, intraday low). **Write "fell roughly 5% to 8% in after-hours trading" or "fell further after hours"; do not give a single precise figure.**

## Context (AI data centres and storage)
- WD sells mostly high-capacity "nearline" hard drives to cloud giants who store AI training data and outputs. Price per terabyte rose ~28% y/y per one call summary (single source: use only as "prices per terabyte rose sharply", or cut).
- WD's CEO said earlier in 2026 the company was "pretty much sold out for calendar 2026" with firm orders from top customers; Seagate also says nearline capacity is fully allocated (Yahoo Finance "Hard drives are sold out for 2026"; WinBuzzer Feb 2026).
- Consumer drive prices have risen in 2026: e.g. ~46% in four months (Sept 2025–Jan 2026) per ThinkComputers; German retail HDD prices up 129% since Sept 2025 per ComputerBase (via secondary). Methods vary: **say "consumer hard-drive prices have risen sharply in 2026", no single %**.

## Reader angle (worldwide)
- Buying an external drive or NAS disk costs more than a year ago, and this report gives no sign of relief soon: WD's best customers are cloud giants, and consumer is only ~5% of its sales.
- Cloud storage (Google Drive, iCloud, OneDrive) is built on these drives; rising costs for providers are worth watching for future plan prices (**speculation: label; no provider has said so**).
- Practical tips the writer can offer: buy only what you need now, compare price per TB, consider a cloud plan you already pay for, back up anyway.
- Investors: strong business, but share price already priced for a lot; volatile.
