## NEW facts (with sources)

### 1. On sale today, in stores and online
- iPhone Duo "made its debut at Apple Store locations worldwide" on Friday 23 October; also sold on apple.com and the Apple Store app. Source: Apple Newsroom, "iPhone Duo arrives at Apple Store locations worldwide" (https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/iphone-duo-arrives-at-apple-store-locations-worldwide/) + Reuters via Dawn (https://www.dawn.com/news/2039239/apple-launches-into-foldable-market-as-iphone-duo-sales-begin). CONFIRMED.
- 28 more countries and regions follow on Friday 30 October (Apple still does not name them in the snippets I saw). Source: Apple Newsroom (arrival page + Sept release). Already in the pre-order story; mention only briefly.
- Price unchanged: from US$1,999 (256 GB). Apple Newsroom Sept release + 9to5Mac. Apple list price only; no Amazon prices.

### 2. Stock: very limited walk-ins, pickup-only markets

Sources: https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/21/heres-where-apple-says-iphone-duo-will-and-wont-be-available-for-walk-ins/ ; https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/21/apple-stores-limited-iphone-duo-for-walk-in/ ; https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/10/21/there-will-be-extremely-limited-iphone-duo-stock-in-store-on-friday . Official-ish (Apple statement quoted by press); 3 independent outlets. CONFIRMED as an Apple statement.
- Walk-in countries (per 9to5Mac's list from Apple): US, Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, Mexico, Germany, Macao, Netherlands, Singapore, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Türkiye, UK, most stores in Italy. MacRumors' list differs slightly. -> Writer: say "the US, UK, Canada, Australia and parts of Europe" rather than the full list.
- Reservation/pickup only (no walk-ins): Reuters (via Dawn) names China, France, Hong Kong, India and Japan. 9to5Mac/others add Malaysia, South Korea, Taiwan, Thailand and the UAE. CONFIRMED for China, France, Hong Kong, India, Japan (Reuters + 9to5Mac). UAE: 9to5Mac + MacRumors (Japan/Korea/UAE notice) — two outlets, Apple localized notice; OK to state.
- Apple's online store told shoppers to "check back" on Saturday 24 October for pickup reservations (MacRumors 21 Oct; Macworld). Two outlets; fine to report as what the store page said.
- Reuters: pre-orders capped at two handsets per customer. Only Reuters found -> attribute to Reuters, or cut.
- India store hours: all six Apple stores in India opened at 8 a.m. on 23 Oct (Business Today, citing Apple's store pages: https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/apple-iphone-duo-sale-begins-today-in-india-prices-store-timings-and-how-to-buy-563097-2026-10-23). Single source -> attribute or cut. US stores opened early (AppleInsider) — attribute.

### 6. Review verdicts (reviews published ~21–22 Oct)
- Overall: positive but qualified. Engadget "a first-class foldable" (https://www.engadget.com/2292333/apple-iphone-duo-review/); Gizmodo "waiting to refine foldables was the right move" (https://gizmodo.com/iphone-duo-review-2000827502); 9to5Mac roundup "the absolute best foldable phone, but not without compromise" (https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/22/iphone-duo-reviews-the-absolute-best-foldable-phone-but-not-without-compromise/); Tom's Guide "best designed foldable" (https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/apple-iphone-duo-review). The Verge harsher: expensive, full of compromises, can be frustrating, but fun (via Macworld/Computerworld roundups). Paraphrase; don't copy sentences.
- Crease: matte nano-texture hides it head-on; still visible at angles and can be felt (Engadget, Gizmodo, PCMag via roundups). CONFIRMED (multiple).
- Battery: mixed. Tom's Guide web-surfing test 13 h 20 min; Engadget video test 28 h 48 min and often 40%+ left at day's end; The Verge says it runs warm and may not last a heavy day. Present as "reviewers disagree". Tests differ, so don't compare numbers directly.
- Weight (254 g) the most common complaint; heavier than iPhone 18 Pro Max (roundups). No telephoto camera; some third-party apps not yet optimised (Apple's own developer news says developers can now submit optimised apps: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/developers-bring-incredible-new-app-experiences-to-iphone-duo/).
- Durability tests: Joanna Stern (WSJ) folded 32,000+ times, hinge held up (Cult of Mac: https://www.cultofmac.com/news/hingey-iphone-duo-hinge-fold-test ; Android Authority roundup). Another tester ~10,000 folds + 1,000 frozen: hinge smooth but crease deepened; small particles could get in (Android Authority: https://www.androidauthority.com/apple-iphone-duo-foldable-display-crease-durability-tests-3726691/ ; Unilad). Robot/hand fold tests are not real-life drops; say so. Still no Apple fold-cycle rating (per pre-order story; nothing new found).

### 7. Repair and AppleCare+ prices (US, from Apple's support listing as reported 14–15 Oct)
- Out of warranty: inner folding display US$999; outer display US$379; outer display + back glass US$509; replaceable top "cover layer" on the inner screen US$49; other accidental damage up to US$1,499. Sources: 9to5Mac (https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/14/heres-how-much-iphone-duo-costs-to-repair-out-of-warranty/), Notebookcheck, Android Authority, Digital Trends, MacObserver. Many outlets citing Apple's support page; I could not open apple.com/support directly. -> Writer: "according to Apple's repair price list, as reported by 9to5Mac". Auditor should open https://support.apple.com/iphone/repair if possible.
- With AppleCare+: inner display US$99, outer display US$29, other accidental damage US$99, cover layer reportedly US$19 (single-ish; cut or attribute); free battery service under 80%; theft/loss deductible US$149.
- AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss for iPhone Duo: US$19.99/month or US$199.99/year (MacObserver: https://www.macobserver.com/news/iphone-duo-applecare-price-19-99-inner-screen-fee/ ; Gadget Hacks; MacDailyNews). A US$399 upfront option is also reported but terms unclear -> cut the US$399.
- Correction to our pre-order story: it said "Repair and AppleCare+ costs are not in Apple's releases". Still true of the press releases, but Apple's support/store listings (reported 14 Oct) did publish them. The new brief can fill this gap; no need to edit the old story unless Pyla wants.

## Reader angle (buyers anywhere)
- If you don't have a pre-order: walking in today is a long shot. In walk-in countries, go early and expect a sell-out; in pickup-only countries (India, UAE, China, Japan, France, Hong Kong, etc.), you need an online reservation, which Apple said to check from 24 Oct. Online orders: think late January.
- Don't pay scalpers; waiting a few weeks also lets more real-world tests appear.
- Budget for protection: an inner-screen repair without AppleCare+ costs about half the phone (US$999 in the US). Check local AppleCare+ prices.
- Reviews say: best-built foldable so far, crease mostly hidden, heavy, battery mixed. Good for people who already want a foldable; most people can wait.
