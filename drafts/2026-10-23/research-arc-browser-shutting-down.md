## Corrections to the brief

- **"Sign-in stops 23 Nov"** applies to **Arc Search on iOS and Android only** (sign-in plus tab sync plus AI features). Desktop sign-in works until accounts are deactivated on 22 Dec. Dia sign-in is not affected.
- **Atlassian ownership: confirmed** (deal closed 21 Oct 2025). The farewell note itself doesn't appear to mention Atlassian, so it's background only.
- Digital Trends wrongly says "December 2022". Ignore it.
- The iOS App Store is **not** confirmed: the company only said the Android app leaves Google Play. Android Authority guesses iOS will follow, which is a guess, so label it or leave it out.

## Open questions (for "the open question" section)

- How long will the basic iOS Arc Search app get security updates after 23 Nov? Not stated.
- Will Arc ever be open-sourced? No promise, only "hope".
- Dia for Windows: is it a finished release or an early build? piunikaweb says the installer was labelled an early build.
- How many people use Arc? No figure has been published. Don't invent one.
- AI browser angle: the company is betting everything on an AI-first browser at a time when OpenAI (Atlas), Perplexity (Comet), Google (Gemini in Chrome) and others are all doing the same. The wider debate is that AI browsers bring new security risks (prompt injection), and the company's own reason for the shutdown is that security work grows in the AI era. For background only: we did not verify claims about rival products in this run, so the writer should keep it general or verify first.

## Reader angle (practical, worldwide)

1. Don't wait. Export now: Settings > Advanced > Export Data (desktop). Also export your bookmarks as an HTML file as a second backup.
2. Moving to Dia? Just sign up and import. Nearly everything comes across, and the AI is optional.
3. Moving elsewhere? A bookmarks HTML file imports into Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox and Zen. Chrome-based browsers keep your Chrome extensions working. Spaces turn into plain folders. Zen (Firefox-based, free) is the closest look-alike, but Chrome extensions don't run there (How-To Geek, supasidebar guides). Community tools (arc-exporter on GitHub) are unofficial: be careful.
4. Save Easels as PNGs. Boosts will be lost.
5. Phone users: AI and sync end 23 Nov. On Android the app goes on 22 Dec.
6. Dates to remember: 23 Nov (mobile), 22 Dec (everything), about 21 Jan 2027 (data deleted).
7. The bigger lesson: when you choose a free app from a startup, your data and habits depend on its owner's plans. Good sign: two months' notice and a built-in export.
