2. **Google's Willow quantum chip "grows" cleaner magic states, cutting a key error source about 40-fold** (science)
   - Google Quantum AI shows "magic state cultivation" (proposed by Craig Gidney, Noah Shutty and Cody Jones in 2024) working on real hardware: errors in magic states fell about 40x, reaching 99.99% logical fidelity (about 1 error in 10,000). Magic states are needed for the extra operations that make a quantum computer universal; the usual method (distillation) can eat most of a machine's qubits. Also moved states from a colour code to a surface code. Published today in Science. Limits: 92% of attempts are thrown away (Duke's Kenneth Brown, via Scientific American); breaking RSA-2048 would need about 1 error in 10 billion, so this is a step, not a threat to today's encryption.
   - Official: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/quantum-computing/magic-state-cultivation-quantum-computing/ ; paper https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aee7961
   - Independent: https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/googles-quantum-willow-chip-may-be-able-to-grow-magic-states/ ; https://www.newscientist.com/article/2593999-google-showcases-way-to-fix-quantum-computer-errors-more-efficiently/ ; https://interestingengineering.com/science/google-willow-quantum-chip-magic-state-errors
