## Careful wording (labelled or cut)

- **What happens to the current plan after a missed QR scan:** the QR section of the help page does NOT say the member drops to Free; it only states the 12-month bar and "choose a different plan". The address section does say fail = switch to free. 9to5Google says members are pushed to Free when the window closes; TechCrunch says they "would instead have to shift to an individual plan". Write: "the page's older address rules say a member who fails loses the plan and moves to the free version; the QR section does not spell this out". MBW flags the same gap.
- **Which countries:** Spotify has not said. Help page text is the same in every regional version checked (US, UK, India, Tonga/"to"), so the rule is written globally; but which markets actually get prompts is unconfirmed. Say "Spotify has not said which countries or how many members".

- **Workarounds (video-call scanning), Reddit reports:** anecdotal, single-type source. Mention only as "some users on Reddit say" if at all; do not give instructions.
