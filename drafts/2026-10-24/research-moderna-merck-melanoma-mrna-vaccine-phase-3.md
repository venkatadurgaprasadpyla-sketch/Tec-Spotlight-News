## What happened (plain words)

On 22 October 2026 (2:59 pm EDT) Merck and Moderna released detailed Phase 3 results for intismeran autogene (also called V940 or mRNA-4157), a personalised mRNA "cancer vaccine" (the companies call it an individualized neoantigen therapy), given with Merck's immunotherapy Keytruda (pembrolizumab) after surgery for high-risk melanoma. The release came after partial results appeared online earlier that day, ahead of the planned ESMO presentation. Full data are due at the ESMO Congress 2026 Presidential Symposium (LBA1) on Saturday 24 October, 4:30 pm CEST (14:30 UTC), presented by Prof. Georgina Long; accepted by the New England Journal of Medicine.

## Facts (two sources each, official first)

| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| Trial: INTerpath-001 (NCT05933577), randomised, double-blind, placebo- and active-controlled, global Phase 3 | Merck release; Cancer Network |
| Patients: 1,137 adults with high-risk (stage IIB–IV) cutaneous melanoma, completely removed by surgery (resected); no prior systemic therapy; randomised 2:1 | Merck release; BioPharm International (Aug topline); Dermatology Times |
| Dosing: intismeran 1 mg every 3 weeks, up to 9 doses, plus Keytruda 400 mg every 6 weeks for about a year; control = placebo plus Keytruda | Merck release; BioPharm International |
| **Primary endpoint: recurrence-free survival (RFS)** = time to any return of the cancer (local, regional or distant) or death from any cause, investigator assessed | Merck release; Cancer Network |
| **Primary result (ITT):** 42% lower risk of recurrence or death, HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.45–0.75), two-sided p<0.001, median follow-up 24.6 months | Merck release; Cancer Network; Dermatology Times |
| Two-year RFS rate 82.2% vs 70.0%. Median RFS not reached in either arm | Merck release; Dermatology Times |
| **Key secondary: distant metastasis-free survival (DMFS):** 38% lower risk, HR 0.62 (0.45–0.85), p=0.002; two-year DMFS 88.5% vs 80.2% | Merck release; Cancer Network; Dermatology Times |
| Benefit consistent across pre-specified subgroups (companies' wording) | Merck release; Cancer Network |
| **Timing (sensitivity analyses, not the primary result):** first 6 months HR 0.99 (no difference); pre-specified sensitivity analysis after 6 months HR 0.42 (58% lower risk); DMFS after 6 months HR 0.50 | Merck release; Cancer Network; Dermatology Times |
| mITT sensitivity analysis (only people who got at least one dose): HR 0.51 | Merck release (single official source; secondary detail; not used) |
| Overall survival: not mature; companies say a "favourable trend" (company claim, no numbers) | Merck release; Yahoo Finance/Investing.com |
| Safety: no new safety signals; grade 3–5 treatment-related side effects 14.2% vs 13.4%; any treatment-related side effects 95.8% vs 90.0%; most common: fever 52.2%, injection-site pain 51.0%, tiredness 38.5% | Merck release; BioSpace (any-TRAE numbers) |
| How it works: made from each patient's tumour sample; encodes up to 34 neoantigens (tumour-specific markers) to train T cells | Merck release; BioPharm International |
| Topline (met RFS and DMFS) first announced 19 August 2026; companies called it the first positive Phase 3 for an individualised neoantigen therapy | Moderna IR insights page; BioPharm International |
| Earlier Phase 2b KEYNOTE-942 (stage IIIB–IV): 5-year HR 0.51 for recurrence or death | BioPharm International; BioSpace (RBC note) |
| **Not approved anywhere.** Merck calls it "investigational"; no filing had been made at topline; companies now "engaging with regulatory authorities" | Merck release; BioPharm International; Moderna IR |
| Keytruda alone is already approved as after-surgery (adjuvant) treatment for resected stage IIB, IIC or III melanoma | Merck release; BioPharm International |
| INTerpath programme: 9 Phase 2 and 3 trials, including 3 Phase 3 in lung cancer (NSCLC) | Merck release; Dermatology Times |
| ESMO Congress 2026, Madrid, 23–27 Oct; LBA1 Saturday 24 Oct 4:30 pm CEST | Moderna release (21 Sep, via Nasdaq); Cancer Network |
| Results accepted by NEJM | Merck release; Cancer Network; Dermatology Times |
| Data appeared online early on 22 Oct, before the official release; Moderna shares fell sharply that day | BioSpace; Yahoo Finance (Investing.com) |

## Health framing

Investigational, not approved anywhere. Results are for adults with high-risk melanoma already removed by surgery, not for preventing melanoma and not for advanced melanoma that can't be operated on. Overall survival not yet known. No medical advice: readers with melanoma should talk to their doctor (and ask about clinical trials).
