Against expectations: adjusted EPS beat (US$0.48 vs about US$0.43-0.45, CNBC and Investing.com). Revenue roughly in line; Tesla's own consensus page had US$28.34 bn and net profit US$828 m, so on Tesla's own numbers revenue and GAAP profit came in slightly below. Say "profit per share beat forecasts; revenue was about in line" and avoid stronger claims.

Why profit fell (Tesla's own explanation, 8-K + Electrek): higher R&D (AI, Optimus, Cybercab), less money from selling regulatory credits, thinner energy margins.

## What is new (products)
- **Optimus robot production has started in Fremont, California** on "high-volume lines"; Tesla calls these its first production-quality bots. CONFIRMED (Update PDF/8-K; Interesting Engineering; Drive Tesla Canada). The Fremont line replaced the old Model S and Model X lines (Q2 8-K).
- **Cybercab (no steering wheel) now carries paying Robotaxi riders in Austin, Texas, since 3 September.** CONFIRMED (Update/8-K; Drive Tesla Canada; Electrek). Cybercab is built at Gigafactory Texas, not Fremont.
- **R&D is now framed around "Super Intelligence" (SI), robotics and energy.** CONFIRMED wording in 8-K; Electrek.

## Guidance and outlook
- **2026 capex cut to about US$20 billion, from "more than US$25 billion".** Two independent reports of the call (Investing.com, Benzinga transcript) plus CNBC-linked coverage; FourWeekMBA says the written Update itself gives no capex guidance. So: a call remark, two sources, OK to use as "Tesla told analysts on the call". No detail on which projects get less.
- **Capex to rise again in 2027 and reach "tens of billions" in 2028** (CFO on call). Investing.com + Benzinga transcripts. OK as a call remark.
- **Optimus ramp will be slow at first**; Musk said the early part of the S-curve "has a shallow slope". Interesting Engineering + Benzinga transcript. OK.
- **Optimus outside customer deliveries around end of 2027.** Two secondary summaries (KuCoin flash, Investing.com transcript); no official line found. Use only softly: "Tesla suggested outside buyers may wait until around the end of 2027".
