## Corrections to the leads

1. **"First big profit"** is too loose. Confirmed: first profitable quarter **since the June 2026 Nasdaq listing**, a swing from a **US$1.1 billion loss a year earlier** (Q3 2025). Q2 2026 was a US$541 million net loss (company's own Q2 release). Do NOT say "first profit ever": we could not confirm SpaceX's full private-company history. Safe wording: "its first profit as a public company".
2. **"Shares up about 10%"**: that was the intraday high on Wed 21 Oct (CNBC headline "jumps 10%"; GuruFocus 9.5% at 9:35 a.m. ET; CoinDesk 9.9% to US$162.41 in the morning). The stock **closed up about 7.8% at US$159.31** (TheStreet; Yahoo/GuruFocus "nearly 8% at $159.31"; CNBC's AMP headline "pops 8%"). Write: "rose as much as 10% on Wednesday and closed almost 8% higher".
3. **"AI unit now its largest"**: true for **revenue only**. Starlink (Connectivity) is still by far the biggest **profit** maker (operating income US$2.37bn vs AI US$431m). Say both.
4. **Revenue precise figure** is US$14.58 billion (rounds to 14.6). Fine as "US$14.6 billion".

### Merger committees (confirmed)

- On the 20 Oct call Musk said both SpaceX and Tesla had formed **special committees of independent directors**, "at the request of shareholders", to study a combination. Sources: CNBC live blog, TechCrunch, NBC News, Axios, CNN, CBS, Fortune.
- Musk is **recused**: quote, "Because I'm a conflicted party, I can't actually be in the discussions of the special committees." (CNBC live blog; Yahoo; Fortune; TheStreet paraphrase.)
- He called the merger "a good idea" (CNN headline; CBS) but said calling it certain would be "overstating it" (Yahoo/Fortune summary).
- **No agreement yet; no guarantee there will be one.** (TechCrunch; NBC; Axios.)
- Tesla approval would need a **majority of all outstanding shares**, not just votes cast; Musk called it "quite a high hurdle" (Fortune; Yahoo; CBS says majority of Tesla shareholders).
- Tesla's seven independent directors (per its 19 Oct proxy) include chair Robyn Denholm; SpaceX committee members not named (Reuters via The Next Web; NBC). Do not claim who sits on Tesla's committee: sources list independent directors, not confirmed committee members.
- Tesla shares: up ~1% after hours on 20 Oct (TechCrunch/NBC); TheStreet says Tesla closed +3.4% at US$386.47 on 21 Oct (**one source; label or cut**).

## Open questions (for the "open question" section)

- Can the AI business stay profitable when it spends about US$16bn a quarter to earn US$7.6bn, with Q4 spending set to roughly double?
