## 1. Verified facts (with source numbers)

### The news peg
- Pre-orders open **Friday 16 October 2026 at 5 a.m. PT** on apple.com and in the Apple Store app. [1]
  - Same moment in other zones (my own conversion, PT is UTC-7 in October): **12:00 UTC, 13:00 UK (BST), 16:00 UAE, 17:30 India (IST)**, 21:00 in South Korea (InvenGlobal also reports 9 p.m. Korea time [12]).
- **In stores and delivered from Friday 23 October 2026**, confirmed on Apple pages (US, UK, India and UAE newsroom releases all say "availability beginning Friday, October 23"). [2][3][4][5]
- Apple's own "get ready" step: shoppers could save their choices to the bag before 9 p.m. PT on Thursday 15 Oct for one-click checkout at launch. [1]
- At the time of checking, **Apple's online store pages for iPhone Duo returned "Service Unavailable" (503)** in the US and UK, which usually means Apple has taken the store down ahead of launch. Not a fault we should report as news. [checked: apple.com/shop/buy-iphone/iphone-duo and /uk/ version]

### What it is (official)
- Apple calls it "the first foldable iPhone". Launched 9 September 2026. [2]
- **Screens:** 7.6-inch inner folding OLED; 5.4-inch outer OLED (measured as a rectangle: 7.58 and 5.36 inches; the viewable area is a little smaller). Apple says the inner screen is 50% larger than iPhone 18 Pro Max's, and the outer screen gives 90% of iPhone 18 Pro's screen area. Both screens share the same shape, so apps scale evenly. ProMotion, Always On, up to 3000 nits outdoors. [1][2][6]
- **Crease approach:** a nano-texture (matte) finish on the inner screen, which Apple says cuts glare and "minimizes crease visibility". Apple does **not** claim the crease is gone. [2]
- **Size and weight:** open 164.6 x 117.8 x 5.2 mm; closed 84.1 x 117.8 x 11.3 mm; **254 g**. Apple calls it its thinnest iPhone ever (when open). [2][6]
- **Build:** grade 5 titanium, mirror-polished; 3D-printed hinge cover; hinge with more than 100 tiny parts and carbon-fibre support plates; glass layers above and below the folding panel that "glide" like book pages to relieve bend stress; titanium plate under the panel. [2]
- **Chip:** A20 Pro (same as iPhone 18 Pro), 2-nanometre process; Apple claims up to 35% better sustained performance than iPhone 17 Pro thanks to a custom vapour chamber. Apple's C2 modem and N1 wireless chip (Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6). [2][6]
- **Battery:** one battery in each half. Apple claims up to 31 hours video on the inner screen, 44 hours on the outer screen, about 24 hours of mixed use. 50% charge in about 20 minutes wired (needs a 60W-capable cable and adapter) or about 30 minutes with MagSafe/Qi2. [2][6]
- **Cameras:** 48MP Fusion main camera with "optical-quality 2x" (a crop of the main sensor, not a separate telephoto lens), 48MP ultra wide, Center Stage front camera, plus a FaceTime camera hidden under the inner screen. 4K120 Dolby Vision video. [1][2]
- **Unlocking:** **Touch ID in the side button**; Face ID is not listed on the spec page. [2][6]
- **SIM:** **eSIM only, worldwide**; no physical SIM slot (dual eSIM). Important for readers in India, the UAE and anywhere physical SIMs are the norm. [2][6]
- **Water and dust:** IP68 (up to 6 m for 30 minutes, lab conditions; liquid damage not covered by warranty). [1][6]
- **Software:** ships with iOS 27.1, with a redesigned layout for the fold, Split View multitasking and StandBy on either screen. Siri AI is in beta, English first; **not available in China**, and **not at first in the EU**. Some cloud AI features have daily limits, with "expanded access ... for a fee in the future". [1][5]
- **Colours:** star white and night sky. [1][2]
- **Accessories:** iPhone Duo Case (sand, navy blue) and iPhone Duo Folio with kickstand (navy blue, taupe), orderable from 16 Oct. UAE prices: AED 319 (case), AED 529 (folio). [1][5]
- **Environment claim:** 35% recycled content overall, 100% recycled cobalt in the battery, 100% recycled titanium in the hinge cover. [2]

### First wave and second wave (official)
- **First wave, 16 Oct pre-order / 23 Oct availability:** "more than 70 countries and regions", including Australia, Brazil, Canada, China, Colombia, France, Germany, India, Japan, Malaysia, Mexico, Singapore, South Korea, Türkiye, the UAE, the UK, the US and Vietnam. [2][3][4][5]
- **Second wave:** 28 more countries and regions from **Friday 30 October**. Apple does not list them in the release. [2]

### Early verdicts (hands-on only; no full reviews found yet)
- **MacRumors (Juli Clover, 9 Sept hands-on):** the crease "is still there, but barely"; visible in some lighting, but Apple hides it better than Samsung; feels premium and sturdy; criticisms: the under-screen camera can be seen on darker screens, and there is no dedicated telephoto lens (2x is a crop). [8]
- **PCMag (exclusive interview, about 12 Oct):** Apple engineers (Dan Murray, VP of silicon engineering, and Banafsheh Barabadi, senior manager of thermal product design) told PCMag that Apple routed a **flexible graphite pipe through the hinge** to move heat between the two halves, so both sides can shed heat. Barabadi said it must survive many hinge cycles. PCMag could not be scraped here; details confirmed via 9to5Mac's write-up and the HN post. [9][10][11]
- **Apple's hardware chief on durability (TechRadar interview, via 9to5Mac, 18 Sept):** Tom Marieb, Apple VP of hardware engineering, said the matte finish is "more forgiving" of hinge changes over time and invited people to "hold us accountable"; he said the hinge torque was tuned to feel solid when closed. **No fold-cycle count** was given. [17]
- **Dave2D (YouTube), reported second-hand:** crease nearly invisible, hinge described as the best on the market. I could not open the video itself; quote only as "according to a summary of Dave2D's hands-on" or skip. [18]
- No independent durability test (fold count, drop test, teardown) found yet.

### Context: foldables already exist
- Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold8 starts at **US$1,899.99 / £1,699** and Fold8 Ultra at **US$2,099.99 / £1,899** (Samsung newsroom, launched 22 July 2026). So the iPhone Duo's US$1,999 sits between them. [19]
- Google sells the **Pixel 11 Pro Fold** (Google Store page live; launched August 2026). I could not confirm its official price on a Google page; skip the price or say "Google also sells a folding Pixel". [20]
- Weight comparison (Duo 254 g vs Fold8 lighter) appears in secondary sources only; Samsung's own weight not checked. Avoid unless checked.

### Rumours and reports (label clearly)
- **Rumour:** analyst Jeff Pu expects very limited iPhone Duo supply at launch and slipping delivery dates (Android Headlines [21]). Not confirmed by Apple.
- Whether pre-orders sell out or ship dates slip will only be known after 12:00 UTC. Do **not** write that it "sold out" unless checked after launch.

### What is still unknown
- Real-world crease and hinge durability over months; Apple has not published a fold-cycle rating.
- Repair cost and AppleCare+ price for the Duo (not in the releases).
- Which 28 countries get it on 30 Oct.
- Official prices for 512 GB to 2 TB outside the US (store down at time of check).
- Supply and delivery times once pre-orders open.
- Full independent reviews (none found yet; likely closer to 23 Oct).

## 4. Cautions for the writer

1. **Pre-orders have not opened at the time of research** (they open 12:00 UTC). Write "opens today" or "opened on 16 October" depending on publish time; never claim it sold out or that ship dates slipped unless checked after launch.
2. **Prices:** only the 256 GB starting prices (US$1,999 / £1,999 / ₹2,99,900 / AED 8,499) are on Apple pages. Add "(checked on 16 October 2026)". Higher tiers are from MacRumors (US) or local press (UK, India, UAE); attribute them or leave them out. **No Amazon prices.** If shop links are added, "Check latest price" only, and no Flipkart for UAE readers.
3. **The 2x camera is a crop**, not a separate telephoto lens. Do not call it a telephoto camera.
4. **Crease:** Apple says the finish "minimizes" crease visibility. Do not say there is no crease. MacRumors: "still there, but barely".
5. **Durability:** no fold-cycle rating from Apple and no independent tests yet. Present Apple's claims as claims ("Apple says").
6. **Battery and performance numbers are Apple's own lab figures** on pre-production units (footnotes in [2]). Say "Apple claims".
7. **eSIM only** worldwide: worth a line for readers in India, the UAE and other places where physical SIMs are common.
8. **Siri AI:** English beta, not in China, not at first in the EU; some cloud AI features have daily limits and may cost extra later. Accurate framing only.
9. **CEO name:** the candidates file says the September release quotes John Ternus as CEO. **I did not verify this** (no quote surfaced in my reads of the release). Do not name Apple's CEO unless the writer checks the release text.
10. **Dave2D** is reported second-hand; either skip or attribute carefully. **PCMag** could not be opened directly; its heat-pipe detail is confirmed through 9to5Mac quoting it. Cite PCMag first, 9to5Mac second.
11. **Competitor prices:** Samsung's are official (newsroom). Do not give a Pixel 11 Pro Fold price (not checked with Google).
12. Do not imply TSMC makes the A20 Pro (Apple's release says only "2-nanometer process"). Linking to the TSMC brief for context on 2nm chips is fine.
13. Don't use Apple's press images as the cover. Apple has an official media kit ("Download media" links on [1] and [2]), but the cover choice for this story is a stock photo with no Apple logo, as asked.
14. Hype words to avoid even though Apple's release uses them: "breakthrough", "magical", "incredible".
