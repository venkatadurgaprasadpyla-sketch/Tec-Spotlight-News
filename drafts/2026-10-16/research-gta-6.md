## 1. What happened (confirmed, official)

- Rockstar Games posted the **Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Trailer on 15 Oct 2026**, with the debut of a new song, "Flower in a Hurricane", by Justice (the French electronic duo who wrote the game's original score) featuring Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. The song is also on "Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album" (pre-save on streaming services; vinyl/CD pre-order at gtavi-thealbum.com).
  - https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/517572321a549a/watch-the-grand-theft-auto-vi-launch-trailer
  - https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/launch-trailer
- Rockstar's own list of GTA VI videos: Trailer 1 (4 Dec 2023, 1:31), Trailer 2 (6 May 2025, 2:47), "An Extended Look" (27 Aug 2026, 26:48, also on Netflix and YouTube), Launch Trailer (15 Oct 2026). Source: https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/launch-trailer and https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/9k2kaa1o3297k9/grand-theft-auto-vi-an-extended-look
- Rockstar's pages show "Plays Best On PS5"; GameSpot and gHacks both say the trailer was captured on a PS5 (GameSpot: a base PS5) and carries PlayStation branding as part of a Sony and Rockstar marketing deal. (Sony–Rockstar partnership wording is GameSpot's, not an official statement I found.)
  - https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-launch-trailer-is-here/ (Eddie Makuch, 15 Oct 2026)
  - https://www.ghacks.net/2026/10/16/rockstar-releases-the-gta-6-launch-trailer-ahead-of-the-november-19-console-release/ (Arthur Kay, 16 Oct 2026)
- What the trailer shows (from gHacks and GameSpot, not official text): more of Jason and Lucia's relationship; Jason talks about leaving the military; Lucia wears an ankle monitor; several helicopter action scenes; it ends with the pair jumping from a roof into a hotel pool. GameSpot notes a "steamy" shower scene. Sources as above.

## 2. Release date and platforms (confirmed, official)

- **Thursday 19 November 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.**
  - Rockstar launch-trailer post (15 Oct 2026): "arrives November 19 to PlayStation 5 and XBOX Series X|S". https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/517572321a549a/watch-the-grand-theft-auto-vi-launch-trailer
  - Rockstar GTA VI site: "Coming November 19, 2026", PS5 / Xbox Series X|S. https://www.rockstargames.com/VI
  - Take-Two's release schedule table (8-K, 7 Aug 2026): Rockstar Games, Grand Theft Auto VI, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, November 19, 2026. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000946581/000162828026054580/ttwo1q27earningsrelease.htm
- **Not on PS4.** PlayStation's official FAQ says it will only be playable on PS5 consoles (PS5 Pro Enhanced). https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/
- **Unlock time (not officially stated in text):** the PlayStation Store data lists the release as 05:00 UTC on 19 Nov for the US store (midnight US Eastern) and 18:30 UTC on 18 Nov for India (midnight IST), which suggests a midnight local-time unlock. Treat as store-listing data, not a Rockstar announcement. (The UK page footer shows "18/11/2026", which looks like a time-zone display quirk; the same page's official text and data say 19 November.)
- **Pre-load:** begins **12 November 2026** (Rockstar Store page; PlayStation lists "7-Day Pre-load"). https://store.rockstargames.com/game/buy-gta-vi

## 3. PC version

- **No PC version has been announced.** Rockstar's pages list only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. gHacks (16 Oct) says Rockstar has not announced a PC version or a date, and the 19 Nov date applies only to consoles.
- gHacks also reports (7 Oct 2026) that **Microsoft said the game will not be playable on PC through cloud streaming**: https://www.ghacks.net/2026/10/07/microsoft-says-gta-6-will-not-stream-exclusively-on-xbox-or-be-playable-on-pc-through-the-cloud/ (I did not open Microsoft's original statement; attribute to gHacks' report.) The Xbox.com page does not mention Cloud Gaming, Play Anywhere or Game Pass.
- **Report (not official):** GameSpot (Darryn Bonthuys, 13 Oct 2026), citing **GamesBeat and unnamed industry sources**, says a PC version could arrive "within the first three months" of 2027. GameSpot itself notes Rockstar hasn't said anything officially. https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-will-reportedly-come-to-pc-much-earlier-than-expected/
- Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has commented on why the game isn't on PC at launch (IGN headline: "Take-Two CEO Explains Why GTA 6 Isn't Coming to PC on Day One"), but I could not open the IGN page (Firecrawl does not support it), so I could not confirm his words or the date. Do not quote him on PC unless the writer finds the original. https://www.ign.com/articles/take-two-ceo-explains-why-gta-6-isnt-coming-to-pc-on-day-one

## 7. Delays and the original 2025 date (official)

1. Trailer 1 (4 Dec 2023) gave a **2025** release (I did not re-open the trailer this run; the BBC and Take-Two's later wording confirm a 2025 target).
2. **16 May 2024:** Take-Two narrowed the window to **fall (autumn) of calendar 2025**, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/technology/take-two-interactive-slashes-fiscal-2025-bookings-forecast-2024-05-16/ ; Take-Two's later results table also lists "Fall of calendar 2025": https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/take-two-interactive-software-inc-reports-results-fiscal-2
3. **2 May 2025:** Rockstar moved the game to **26 May 2026** ("We are very sorry that this is later than you expected"). https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/258aa538o412ok/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-coming-may-26-2026
4. **6 Nov 2025:** Rockstar moved it to **Thursday 19 Nov 2026**, apologising for the extra wait and saying the time would let it finish the game "with the level of polish you have come to expect and deserve". https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026
- BBC (updated 15 Oct 2026) also summarises: delayed twice, from autumn 2025 and May 2026. https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c9q20djz4wno
- Total slip from the original autumn 2025 window: about one year.

## 9. Setting and lead characters (Rockstar's official text only)

- Setting: **"Vice City, USA."** Rockstar calls it "the darkest side of the sunniest place in America", inside **the state of Leonida**. The 6 Nov 2025 post speaks of "the sprawling state of Leonida and a return to modern day Vice City".
- Story set-up (summarise, do not copy): **Jason and Lucia** have always known the odds are against them; when an easy job goes wrong, they get caught in a criminal conspiracy that runs across Leonida and must rely on each other to survive.
- Full names appear on official store pages: **Jason Duval** and **Lucia Caminos** (Xbox.com page keywords; Rockstar Store screenshot captions "Jason Duval", "Lucia Caminos"). Other named characters in Rockstar Store screenshot captions: Boobie Ike, Cal Hampton, Brian Heder; places: Leonida Keys, Grassrivers.
  - https://www.rockstargames.com/VI ; https://store.rockstargames.com/game/buy-gta-vi ; https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/grand-theft-auto-vi
- Official rating: ESRB **M (Mature 17+)** (blood and gore, intense violence, nudity, strong sexual content, drug and alcohol use, in-game purchases); **PEGI 18** on the UK PlayStation Store. Important for the reader angle (not for children). PlayStation's FAQ points parents to the PS Family App.

## 11. Why it matters (context)

- GTA V (2013) has sold-in over 230 million units, and the series nearly 475 million (Take-Two, 7 Aug 2026, via Game Developer and VGChartz). It is one of the best-selling games ever, across three console generations.
- Take-Two is leaning on GTA VI for a record year (US$8.0–8.2 billion bookings outlook).
- Worldwide reader angle: trending in India (Google Trends, per candidates.md); prices in India are Rs 5,999 / Rs 7,499 on PlayStation; console-only at launch, so PC players (a large share of players in India and elsewhere) must wait; physical boxes contain only a code, so a decent internet connection is needed either way; the free GTA+ month auto-renews.
- Open questions for the brief: PC date; whether there will be an online mode and when; file size; whether the 19 Nov date holds (two delays already, though five weeks out with the launch trailer and pre-load date set, a further delay looks unlikely; that is a judgment, not a fact).
