## 1. What Waymo itself said (official blog)

Source: Waymo blog, "Hello Toronto! Waymo is En Route", 16 Oct 2026, https://waymo.com/blog/2026/10/waymo-in-toronto/ (English and French text)

- Waymo says it is "officially heading to Canada", laying groundwork for its fully autonomous ride-hailing service in Toronto, with the **goal** of welcoming first riders **in 2028** (a goal, not a firm date).
- "In the coming days" Waymo cars will appear on Toronto streets, **driven manually**. Phased approach: first high-definition mapping of the street network, then testing with trained autonomous specialists behind the wheel. Waymo calls this a "validation period" so the Driver adapts to Toronto's conditions.
- Quote, co-CEO **Tekedra Mawakana**: expanding into Canada is "an important milestone in Waymo's global journey"; Toronto called "one of the world's great cities" and "Canada's largest city".
- **Element Fleet Management**: Toronto-headquartered fleet and mobility company; will provide "end-to-end fleet management services". Quote from Element CEO **Laura Dottori-Attanasio** ("As a Canadian company, we are proud to be expanding our partnership with Waymo..."). Note the word "expanding": Element was already a Waymo partner before this (Electrek's Sept 2024-era image filename "Waymo Element" hints at an earlier US deal; not checked, so do not state details).
- **Jobs**: "Together with Element and our partners, we'll invest in hundreds of roles over time" in fleet maintenance, validation driving, customer service and other operations. No number, no timeline beyond "over time".
- Existing Canadian ties: **Canada Pension Plan Investment Board** (long-term investor) and **Magna International** (manufacturing partner).
- Weather: links to its "all-weather Driver" post; says Waymo can be "an additional option" alongside bike lanes and transit.
- Safety claim (Waymo's own): "over 400 million fully autonomous kilometres" and "95% fewer (20x fewer) crashes involving serious injury or worse compared to humans in the cities where we operate". Links to waymo.com/safety.
- Accessibility partners: BALANCE for Blind Adults, CNIB, Community Living Toronto, Connected Canadians, Epilepsy Ontario. Quote from BALANCE CEO Michelle McQuigge.
- Sign-up page: waymo.com/waymo-in-canada.
- **Not in the blog**: no permit, no vehicle type, no neighbourhoods, no price, no fleet size.

## 2. Ontario pilot permit and the rules

- Waymo says it has been **approved for Ontario's Automated Vehicle Pilot Program** and has a **permit from Ontario's Ministry of Transportation** to begin manual driving and testing; says it will keep working with regulators on permits for each phase. Testing typically runs 24/7. Starts with a **limited number of all-electric Jaguar I-PACE** vehicles. (Financial Post, Denise Paglinawan, via Yahoo Finance Canada, 16 Oct 2026: https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/waymo-plans-launch-self-driving-141021771.html ; it attributes this to Waymo's Canada page https://waymo.com/waymo-in-canada/ , which I did not scrape.)
- Canadian Press (via CP24, 16 Oct 2026): Waymo says the Ontario government gave it the green light to join the provincial pilot. Mapping and driver-behind-the-wheel testing start "this fall". https://www.cp24.com/local/toronto/2026/10/16/waymo-to-launch-robotaxis-in-toronto-this-is-how-the-pilot-program-works/
- Test neighbourhoods (Financial Post): St. Lawrence Market, Harbourfront, Roncesvalles Village, Parkdale, Liberty Village, Don Mills, Yorkdale, Leaside, East York, Summerhill, Yonge & Eglinton, Weston, Mimico and Etobicoke.
- The rule itself: Ontario's pilot regulation is **O. Reg. 306/15** (automated vehicle testing since 2016; driverless testing added in 2019, per Electrek). An amendment, **O. Reg. 268/25** (filed 27 Nov 2025), sets the pilot to be **revoked on 13 October 2027**, before Waymo's 2028 target. Sources: https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/r25268 (found via search; Firecrawl scrape of e-Laws did not return the text, so I have **not read the clause myself**) and Electrek (below), which says the same.
- Electrek also reports Transport Canada told Canadian Affairs that self-driving cars on public roads are limited to "temporary pilot projects" run by provinces, and that the pilot is a **testing** framework, not a ride-hailing licence. So paid rides in 2028 need new provincial rules. (Electrek, Fred Lambert, 16 Oct 2026: https://electrek.co/2026/10/16/waymo-toronto-canada-robotaxi-2028/)
- Ontario programme background page: https://www.ontario.ca/page/automated-vehicle-pilot-program (not re-checked today).
- Not the first robot vehicles under the pilot: Gatik began rolling out 50 autonomous box trucks for Loblaw in the city last year (Canadian Press via CP24).

## 4. The 95% safety claim (what it really is)

- Source: Waymo Safety Impact dashboard, https://waymo.com/safety/impact/ (scraped today): data **through June 2026**, **271.3 million rider-only miles** (about 436 million km, so "over 400 million km" matches) in **Phoenix (92.1M), San Francisco Bay Area (82.4M), Los Angeles (67.1M), Austin (21.1M), Atlanta (8.6M)**.
- Serious-injury-or-worse crash rate: Waymo **0.01** vs human benchmark **0.21** incidents per million miles, all locations, which is the 95% / "20x" figure. Dashboard also says 55 fewer such crashes; 82% fewer injury crashes (841 fewer); 93% fewer pedestrian injury crashes; 86% fewer cyclist injury crashes.
- Method: Waymo's crashes come from US **NHTSA Standing General Order** reports; human benchmarks from police-reported data with adjustments; method follows Waymo's own peer-reviewed papers **Kusano et al. 2024** (Traffic Injury Prevention, "Comparison of Waymo Rider-only crash data to human benchmarks at 7.1 million miles", https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/15389588.2024.2380786) and **Kusano et al. 2025** (Traffic Injury Prevention, doi 10.1080/15389588.2025.2499887). These are written by Waymo researchers. So: it is **Waymo's own analysis**, published in a peer-reviewed journal, updated quarterly; it is not an independent audit.
- Electrek (24 Sep 2026) summarised the June update the same way: 271M miles through June, 82% fewer injury crashes, 95% fewer serious-injury-or-worse. https://electrek.co/2026/09/24/waymo-says-it-has-stopped-841-injuries-in-271-million-autonomous-miles/
- Independent check: Electrek says IIHS found Waymo safer than human drivers by smaller margins, "with some caveats" (https://electrek.co/2026/07/25/waymo-is-2-3-safer-than-a-human-driver-says-iihs-with-some-caveats/ ; not read, headline only). Waymo's safety page also cites a Swiss Re insurance-claims study (older, 25M miles).
- Key caveat for the story: **none of these miles are in a Canadian city or in heavy snow**; the five cities are mostly warm.

## 5. Snow and winter: what Waymo has done so far

- Waymo blog "Creating an all-weather Driver" (Oct 2025), https://waymo.com/blog/2025/10/creating-an-all-weather-driver/ : Waymo says it already handles rain, fog, sandstorms and freezing temperatures, and is working on snow. It says it has driven **"tens of thousands of miles"** in snowy conditions, regularly testing in **Upstate New York, Michigan's Upper Peninsula and the Sierra**. Sensors (cameras, radar, lidar) have heated cleaning systems. Its AI can tell snow, slush, ice and dry road apart and adjusts speed and braking. Validation via real driving in **Detroit, Denver and Washington DC**, closed courses and simulation. Service rules will set when cars operate based on local conditions. (So: no Buffalo specifically; "Upstate New York" is Waymo's wording.)
- History: Waymo began winter testing on Michigan roads in 2017 and has had a Novi, Michigan, engineering site since 2016 (Fortune 2017, https://fortune.com/2017/10/26/alphabet-waymo-self-driving-cars-snow ; The Drive). Search results only; not scraped.
- Denver, Waymo's first big snow city: Waymo said in July 2026 it would begin fully driverless operation in Denver within weeks, employees first, public later in 2026; Colorado regulators said in March they want to see how the cars handle ice and snow before approving full commercial service (Hoodline, Denver Gazette, Colorado Sun via search results; not scraped). Electrek (16 Oct) calls Denver "Waymo's first real snow city". I found **no public results yet** on how Waymo did in a full snowy winter with no driver.
- Detroit: announced for 2026 with supervised testing first (DBusiness, Gizmodo, via search). Status not confirmed.
